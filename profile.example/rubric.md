## Experience gate
Roles requiring 0-3 years of experience, including new-grad and unlisted.

## Score: 0-100 = realistic likelihood of landing the role
- Role/domain fit (0-35): your core area = full marks; adjacent ML = partial.
- Seniority match (0-25): new-grad or 0-2 yrs = full; "1-3 years"/unlisted = ~18.
- Degree fit (0-15): full by default; a PhD requirement is a red flag, not a deduction.
- Company/team competitiveness (0-15): weigh attainability, not only brand.
- Logistics (0-10): location and start date compatible = full.

Anchors: 85-100 exceptional; 60-84 strong; 40-59 a stretch; <40 skip.
